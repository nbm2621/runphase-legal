What Runphase does

Runphase runs a planned change against a Jira issue. You write the procedure, work through it step by step while the change is happening, record what you observed, and are left with an immutable record of what was done, by whom, and when.

What Runphase does not do

People use this tool while production changes are underway, so the boundary is worth setting out in full.

  • It does not perform your change. Runphase will not connect to your infrastructure, run commands, deploy anything, or alter any system other than its own records. A person carries out every step.
  • It does not verify that a step worked. Marking a step complete, or a success criterion passed, records your assertion. The app has no way to check it, and does not try.
  • It does not enforce your change management process, approve changes, or amount to approval. Whether a change may proceed is decided outside this app.
  • It does not certify compliance. The record it produces may be useful evidence in an audit. Whether it satisfies any particular standard, regulation or internal policy is yours to determine.
  • It is not a monitoring or alerting system. There are no server-side timers. If a change window is exceeded, that gets noticed by a browser which happens to be open and confirmed against the app's own clock, not by a watchdog that will page anyone.
  • It is not a secrets store. The Privacy Policy covers that.
