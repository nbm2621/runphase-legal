What Runphase does

Runphase runs a planned change against a Jira issue. You author a procedure, execute it step by step while the change is happening, record what you observed, and are left with an immutable record of what was done, by whom, and when.

What Runphase does not do

Stated plainly, because this is a tool used during production changes and the boundary matters:

  • It does not perform your change. Runphase does not connect to your infrastructure, run commands, deploy anything, or alter any system other than its own records. Every step is carried out by a person.
  • It does not verify that a step worked. When you mark a step complete or a success criterion passed, Runphase records your assertion. It has no way to check it and does not try.
  • It does not enforce your change management process, approve changes, or constitute approval. Whether a change may proceed is decided outside this app.
  • It does not certify compliance. The record it produces may be useful evidence for an audit; whether it satisfies any particular standard, regulation or internal policy is yours to determine.
  • It is not a monitoring or alerting system. There are no server-side timers. If a change window is exceeded, that is noticed by a browser that happens to be open and confirmed by the app's own clock — not by a watchdog that will page anyone.
  • It is not a secrets store. See the Privacy Policy.

Your responsibilities

  • Decide whether a change is safe to make. Runphase records a procedure; it does not review one.
  • Keep credentials and other secrets out of the text you enter. The app screens for them and can redact afterwards, but neither is a guarantee — see the Privacy Policy for what redaction does and does not reach.
  • Manage who can see what through Jira. Runphase shows a person only the runs whose Jira issue they could already open, which means your Jira permissions are the control. The app adds no barrier of its own and removes none.
